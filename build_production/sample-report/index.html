About Diego

Meet Diego Cano

For Diego Cano, home inspections became more than a career—they became a way to build a life centered around both family and service. As a Certified Master Inspector with more than 15,000 inspections completed throughout Florida, Diego has dedicated his career to helping homeowners make informed decisions about one of their largest investments.

As a single father, the flexibility of the inspection industry allowed him to remain actively involved in his son’s life while continuing to build a business founded on integrity, professionalism, and trust. That experience reinforced something he already believed: a home is more than just a building—it’s where families create memories, find security, and build their future.

As the son of immigrant parents who came to this country in pursuit of a better future, Diego learned early that success is built through consistency, accountability, and a willingness to outwork the obstacles in front of you. Those principles helped shape both his career and the culture of Calidad Inspections, where integrity, service, and hard work remain at the center of everything we do.

After years of experience inspecting homes throughout Florida, Diego recognized that behind every transaction is a family looking for answers, confidence, and peace of mind. That’s why our mission goes beyond identifying defects. We strive to educate our clients, help them understand their property, and provide the information they need to make confident decisions.

Every inspection is approached as if we were inspecting a home for our own family because we understand what is truly at stake.

FAQ

Quick answers before scheduling.

These questions help visitors understand the process and contact the company faster.

How long does a home inspection usually take?

Most inspections take approximately 2 to 4 hours depending on the size, condition and complexity of the property.

Do you provide same-day reports?

Same-day digital reports are available for many inspections. Timing can vary depending on the property and report type.

What areas do you serve?

Calidad Inspections serves South Florida, including Broward, Miami-Dade and Palm Beach.

What is included in a 4-Point Inspection?

A 4-Point Inspection focuses on four major systems: roof, electrical, plumbing and HVAC.

Can real estate agents schedule inspections for clients?

Yes. Realtors can call, text or use the contact form to request scheduling support for their clients.
